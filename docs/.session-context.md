## Decisions & Context

- **Session workflow (Nathan's directive): implementation subagents scoped per
  workstream + adversarial review subagents once work completes.** It paid for
  itself twice: the WS4 reviewer's 60k-world simulation caught geometry defects
  two visual checks missed, and the WS5 reviewer refuted a wrong theory of mine
  before I "fixed" a non-bug.
- **Keep-out geometry:** the phyllotaxis annulus is effectively a zero-width
  RING (`CLUSTER_RADIUS` 0.55 < `CENTER_CLEAR_FRAC` 0.59 → all centers at
  r≈0.59). Ring capacity ≈ 3 displaced tiles clear of ONE rect; two rects
  exceed it at aspect ≤1.5. Hence the both-bands gate (deck rect only).
  Design options if ever needed: widen the annulus (2D freedom) or gate bands
  by aspect. Album-only Worlds clump at ~1:1 viewports — HR-6 territory.
- **`?bandx`/`?bandy` nudges re-key the keep-out on the NEXT build**, not per
  frame (accepted; rect reads `BAND_TUNABLES` at build time).
- **worldLive `assign()` contract:** the pool promise NEVER rejects on stream
  failure — only on slot reassign/release. A hung stream blocks steady-state
  rotation until the next Turn (pre-existing, known, low-priority).
- **Turn-gap probes are noisy headless** (parallel HLS startup lands in the
  window); the build-stall signature (consistent big gap AT the click) is what
  matters, and it's absent. Real-device feel is Nathan's gate.
- **Live URL dials now:** `?fieldy ?spready ?spreadx ?max ?min ?vtiles
